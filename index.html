What I offer

I work flexibly across strategy, solution development, implementation and capability, depending on where support is most useful.

Strategic Design

Defining the problem, assessing whether and how EO and related technologies can help, and identifying the choices and trade-offs. This includes aligning technology with your mission and measurable outcomes, independent technical reviews, and supporting adoption centred on user needs.

Typical engagements & outputs

A typical engagement might involve: a focused independent review; workshops and stakeholder discussions to clarify needs and options; development of a strategy or technology roadmap; or strategic advice to help shape an initiative or technology direction.

Outputs might include: an independent assessment, options and recommendations, or practical next steps, documented in a brief or report where appropriate; or a roadmap.

Solution Development

Designing and prototyping sensor-agnostic solutions that combine EO, AI and cloud technologies where they add clear value, with scalability, operational needs and existing capability considered from the outset.

Typical engagements & outputs

A typical engagement might involve: translating a defined need into a technical approach; assessing and selecting suitable data, methods and technologies; developing and testing a proof of concept or prototype; or reviewing and refining an existing solution design.

Outputs might include: a solution design or technical design report; a prototype or working workflow with code and documentation; or an implementation plan outlining the steps, requirements and resources needed to move into operation.

Operational Implementation

Advising and supporting technical teams to scale, automate and optimise EO workflows for performance and cost, with the operational context and targeted capability development considered throughout.

Typical engagements & outputs

A typical engagement might involve: reviewing a prototype or existing workflow for operational readiness; advising on approaches to scaling and automation; identifying performance, cost and integration issues; working with technical teams through implementation; or identifying and addressing specific capability gaps needed for ongoing operation.

Outputs might include: an operational readiness assessment; recommendations for scaling, automation and optimisation; implementation guidance or a transition plan; and targeted capability development, technical documentation or knowledge transfer.

Capability & Program Support

Providing strategic and technical leadership, capability development and program support across complex initiatives, helping build sustainable EO capability that fits the organisation and its context.

Typical engagements & support

A typical engagement might involve: providing part-time strategic or technical leadership; supporting the design, establishment or delivery of an EO program or initiative; providing independent technical advice across a program; developing team or organisational capability; or helping coordinate work across technical teams, partners and stakeholders.

Outputs and support might include: program or capability plans; technical or strategic advice and reviews; capability assessments and development activities; program documentation and decision support; or ongoing advisory support as the initiative evolves.

Remote Sensing

I primarily work with multispectral optical sensors due to their widespread availability, but I'm constantly exploring new technologies driven by curiosity and application needs. To construct the best model of Earth, we need measurements from as many perspectives as possible. The development of the first analysis-ready synthetic aperture radar dataset and exploration of hyperspectral data for landscape characterisation are just some examples of my work.

Turning Data into Insights

The value of data lies in the insights it reveals. My professional career started with surveying the sky, studying powerful cosmic explosions to understand the history of the universe. Today, I analyse Earth observation data to map natural resources and track environmental changes. I take a purposeful approach, starting with the real question and balancing accuracy with practicality.
